Several aging-associated pathways are evolutionarily conserved across species. However, whether common cellular aging states exist across evolutionarily divergent species remains unclear. We aim to characterize conserved aging-related features at single-cell resolution in *Drosophila* and investigate the extent to which these features converge within individual cells. Ultimately, we seek to identify evolutionarily conserved cellular aging programs that may represent fundamental features of cellular aging across species.
