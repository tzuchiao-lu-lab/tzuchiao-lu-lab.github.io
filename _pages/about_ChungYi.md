### **梁中怡 Chung-Yi Liang, PhD, Research Scientist**
    
Chung-Yi earned her PhD from National Taiwan University, where she studied transcriptional regulation in yeast. She subsequently joined Allen Ao-Lin Hsu’s lab to investigate aging using *C. elegans*. In 2022, she became involved in a research collaboration between the laboratories of Allen Hsu and Hongjie Li. In 2026, she joined the Lu Lab as a research scientist, focusing on aging research in flies and worms. Outside the lab, she enjoys cooking and exploring delicious food!
