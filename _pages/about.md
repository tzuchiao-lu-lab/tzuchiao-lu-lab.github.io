#### **Decode cellular aging heterogeneity across species, individuals, and age-related diseases.**

Aging reshapes cells and tissues in highly heterogeneous ways. Our lab develops experimental and computational approaches to profile these changes at cellular resolution, with a particular emphasis on whole-organism single-cell genomics. We investigate three major questions: What cellular aging mechanisms are conserved across species? Why do individuals age differently? And how does cellular aging contribute to age-related diseases? By integrating comparative biology, single-cell genomics, and computational approaches, we aim to uncover both shared and context-specific principles of cellular aging.

#### **解析跨物種、個體與老化相關疾病中的細胞老化異質性**

老化會以高度異質的方式重塑不同細胞與組織。我們實驗室致力於發展實驗與計算方法，以細胞解析度描繪這些老化相關變化，並特別著重於全生物體單細胞基因體學（whole-organism single-cell genomics）。我們主要探討三個核心問題：哪些細胞老化機制在不同物種間具有保守性？為什麼不同個體具有不同的老化軌跡？以及細胞老化如何影響老化相關疾病？ 透過整合比較生物學、單細胞基因體學與計算分析，我們希望揭示細胞老化中共享且具情境特異性的基本原則。
